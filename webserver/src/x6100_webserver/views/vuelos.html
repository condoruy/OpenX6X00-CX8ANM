Flights

The aircraft over the chosen area, as on Flightradar: the same data as APP → Flights on the radio. It comes from open networks of volunteers’ ADS-B receivers (airplanes.live, adsb.lol): only the aircraft some nearby receiver picks up are seen. It updates every 15 seconds. Tap the map to centre it there.

Listen tower plays the air traffic control of the airport here in the browser, over the internet. While it plays, the aircraft most likely talking to that tower (near the airport and low: approaching, departing or on the ground) are highlighted in blue with their registration.

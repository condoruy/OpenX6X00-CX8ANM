Weather

The weather and the alerts for where the radio is, the same as APP → Weather. The position comes from the GPS if it has one, else from the QTH locator and else from the capital of the country chosen. The official alerts come from the country’s service (INUMET in Uruguay, SMN in Argentina, INMET in Brazil, NWS in the United States, MeteoAlarm in Europe); in the other countries they are notices calculated from the forecast, which are not official.

Each discharge on battery, from the radio's logs: how long it lasted, what drew on the pack and how the % of our firmware compares with the % of the radio's base. The base measures the voltage but no current, so what each thing drew is an estimate.

Battery test

To measure the pack, once

  1. Charge the battery full with the radio off, until the charger stops.
  2. Unplug the supply and switch the radio on. Use it as usual: receive, WiFi, Bluetooth, whatever you do every day.
  3. Leave it on until it switches itself off for lack of battery. Do not plug the supply in between.
  4. Plug the supply in, switch on and open this page: the discharge appears at the top. Download the report to send it.

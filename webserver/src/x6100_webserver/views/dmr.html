DMR

Listen to DMR talkgroups over the internet on the radio: APP → DMR. The radio joins the network as a hotspot of its own and only listens: it never transmits on DMR. The voice comes out of the speaker (or Bluetooth headphones).

Before you start

Once, in three steps

  1. Get a DMR ID (7 digits) from radioid.net, if you do not have one yet. It is free; they check your licence.
  2. In BrandMeister SelfCare (log in with your DMR ID): Profile → Hotspot Security: turn it on and choose a password. That is the hotspot password below.
  3. The radio receives all the talkgroups of your list at once, and on the radio (APP → DMR) you mark which ones you hear: all or only some. In BrandMeister they must be static talkgroups of the hotspot: the easiest is to create an API key (Profile → API Keys) and paste it below, and the radio adds them for you. Without the key, add them yourself in SelfCare, under My hotspots (the radio appears there with your DMR ID and two more digits, the SSID).

FreeDMR and other networks of hotspots work too: there the radio asks for all the talkgroups of the list when it connects.

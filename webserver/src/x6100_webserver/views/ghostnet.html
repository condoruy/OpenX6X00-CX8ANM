GhostNet

GhostNet is a set of HF nets for passing information without depending on the internet or mobile phones, mostly with JS8. On the radio: APP → Modes → JS8 → Freq → GhostNet mode (7.107 MHz and the groups). The official nets are in English. For them the radio translates the messages that come in and, if you want, yours before they go out (with WiFi). There is no GhostNet net in your language yet: you can set up your own below.

How to take part

A summary of GhostNet version 1.5.

  1. Listen first. In JS8 choose Freq → GhostNet mode: the radio goes to 7.107 MHz and joins @GHOSTNET, @GSTFLASH and your regional group. Listening needs no licence; transmitting does.
  2. Groups. @GHOSTNET for everyday traffic; @GSTFLASH for emergencies only; @GN + country + state for your area (for example @GNURYMO is Montevideo).
  3. Checking in. In the net’s window send a short message to the group, for example @GHOSTNET QRV MONTEVIDEO GF15 ALL OK. Pick a free offset; heartbeats go below 1000 Hz.
  4. Power. Just enough: if you have a lot, turn it down so you don’t cover those who transmit with little.
  5. RTTY. An hour after each net starts there are broadcasts on 7.077 MHz at 45.45 baud for whoever could not check in: JS8 → Freq → GhostNet: nets, translation, RTTY → RTTY.
  6. Voice. 7.190 MHz LSB only in serious emergencies, in the last 5 minutes of the window.
  7. No encryption. On the amateur bands messages may not be encrypted: everything goes in the clear and with your callsign.

Winlink over VARA and Ion2G ALE are PC programs: they don’t run inside the radio, but you can use them with a PC connected. The official document: github.com/s2underground/GhostNet.

Zello

Zello channels on the radio: APP → Zello. You listen on the speaker (or Bluetooth headphones) and talk with the radio’s side PTT or the Talk button, through the hand microphone without pressing its PTT. What you say goes to Zello, not on the air.

Zello account

The same as in the phone app

Developer keys

Zello asks everyone to use their own keys (they are free):

  1. Go to developers.zello.com with your Zello user name and password.
  2. Fill in the developer profile and save it.
  3. Keys → Add Key. Copy the Issuer and the Private Key whole (with the BEGIN and END lines) and paste them here.

The radio signs its own connection permit with this key, so the radio’s clock has to be right (with internet it sets itself).

Channels

Up to 20, one a line, written as in Zello
